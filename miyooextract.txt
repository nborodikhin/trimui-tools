Utility to extract root filesystem from a Miyoo Flip firmware image
Tested on Linux

Use --xz to produce rootfs.img.tar.xz instead of rootfs.img.tar.gz.
Requires xz in addition to the default dependencies.

Official firmware and toolchains: https://lomiyoo.com/pages/firmware
See also: https://github.com/Zetarancio/Miyoo-Flip-Mainline-Linux-Reverse-Engineering
